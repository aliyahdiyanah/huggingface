How is this calculated?

Positivity score = P(positive) + 0.5 × P(neutral), using the probabilities from a 3-class RoBERTa sentiment model (negative / neutral / positive) running in your browser. A fully neutral message scores 50%.
Wellbeing index = the average positivity score of your last 5 messages.
The score and mood label are also passed to the AI counselor so its reply matches your emotional state.
